# HR Assistant

## Company
Prime Focus Group is a diversified industrial group operating across HVAC, MEP, fabrication, and manufacturing in the United Arab Emirates. We are looking for a detail-oriented HR Assistant to support our people operations team in Dubai.

## About the Role
The HR Assistant provides day-to-day administrative and coordination support to the HR department, with a focus on UAE labour and payroll administration, employee records, and onboarding. This is an assistant/coordinator-level role reporting to the HR Manager.

## Responsibilities
- Support end-to-end HR administration including maintaining employee records and HR files
- Assist with monthly payroll preparation, attendance, and leave administration
- Coordinate onboarding and offboarding of employees, including documentation and induction
- Support UAE labour law compliance, visa/immigration paperwork, and WPS processing
- Maintain and update the HRIS and generate basic HR reports
- Assist with recruitment coordination: scheduling interviews and candidate communication
- Respond to employee queries on HR policies, benefits, and grievances
- Provide general administrative support to the HR and welfare functions

## Required Skills and Qualifications
- 2+ years of experience in an HR administrative or coordinator role, preferably in an industrial, construction, or manufacturing environment
- Working knowledge of UAE labour law and payroll administration
- Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, Word, Outlook)
- Experience with an HRIS or payroll system
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
- Fluency in English

## Preferred Skills and Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field
- Experience supporting a large blue-collar or site-based workforce
- Knowledge of WPS, visa processing, and Emiratization requirements
- Additional languages (e.g., Hindi, Tagalog, Arabic) for workforce communication
- UAE-based with the ability to join immediately
