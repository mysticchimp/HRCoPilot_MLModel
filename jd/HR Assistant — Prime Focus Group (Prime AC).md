### **HR Assistant — Prime Focus Group (Prime AC)**

**Location:** Dubai, UAE (on-site) · **Reports to:** HR Manager · **Function:** Human Resources · **Level:** Assistant / junior (this is a hands-on admin seat, not a supervisory role)

**Role summary**  
 Support the full employee lifecycle for a UAE HVAC/ductwork manufacturing operation spanning a factory workforce and office staff — visa and labour-card coordination, WPS payroll support, records, onboarding, and recruitment coordination across the group's open roles.

**Key responsibilities**

* Coordinate visa, labour card, Emirates ID, and medical processing via PRO/typing centre (Tasheel, MOHRE, GDRFA portals); track expiries and renewals.  
* Support monthly payroll and WPS submissions; maintain attendance, overtime, and leave records for factory and office staff.  
* Maintain employee files and HRIS records (Bayzat / ZenHR) — joiners, transfers, exits, end-of-service/gratuity data.  
* Run onboarding/offboarding logistics: offer letters, MOHRE contract registration, inductions, asset issue/return.  
* Coordinate recruitment admin across the group's open roles — screening logistics, interview scheduling, candidate correspondence, agency liaison.  
* Handle first-line employee queries; keep HR compliant with UAE Labour Law and MOHRE requirements.

**Must-have**

* 1–4 years HR/admin experience in the UAE.  
* Working knowledge of UAE Labour Law, WPS, and visa/labour-card processes.  
* HRIS familiarity (Bayzat, ZenHR, or similar) and strong MS Office/Excel.  
* Fluent English; strong records discipline and confidentiality.

**Nice-to-have**

* Experience in an industrial-sector employer: manufacturing, HVAC, MEP, construction, contracting, facilities management, or building materials (blue-collar / labour-camp workforce exposure).  
* Arabic language.  
* Tagalog/Filipino, to support the Filipino factory workforce.  
* CIPD or equivalent HR certification.  
* Hands-on PRO / government-portal experience (Tasheel, MOHRE, GDRFA).
